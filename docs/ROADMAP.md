# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — playable 3D foundation (implemented)
- Procedural stadium/field
- 11-v-11 live simulation
- Passing/running/play calling
- Smart blocking and defensive pursuit
- Scoring/downs/clock
- Persistent career roster
- Lv.100, rebirth and 10★ progression

## Phase 2 — richer football
- Route editor and audibles
- Better line interaction / pocket formation
- Zone shells, man coverage assignments and blitz packages
- Catch animations, contested catches and tipped balls
- Fumbles and live recoveries
- Kick meter, wind and returns
- Penalties and timeouts
- Replay camera and instant replay
- Controller/mobile touch controls

## Phase 3 — complete career
- 17-game schedule and standings
- Playoffs/championship bracket
- Draft classes and scouting
- Free agents, trades, salary cap and contracts
- Staff hiring/coordinator traits
- Morale/condition/injuries
- Dilemmas, news and random events
- Records, achievements and Hall of Fame
- Team/uniform editor

## Phase 4 — presentation
- Original character models and animation set
- Original crowd/stadium/audio package
- Weather, day/night and field conditions
- Broadcast camera package
- Accessibility options and difficulty sliders
- Save export/import and multiple career slots
